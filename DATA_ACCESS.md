# PT-3 Dataset Access

PT-3 includes 733 main thermogram–mask pairs and 80 external validation pairs. To request access, email [liuhuarui@mail.ustc.edu.cn](mailto:liuhuarui@mail.ustc.edu.cn?subject=PT-3%20Dataset%20Access%20Request) from your institutional address.

**Subject** `PT-3 Dataset Access Request`

- **Name and institution**
- **Intended use** — Briefly describe your project and specify the main dataset, external validation subset or both.
- **Ethics document** — Attach the ethics approval covering the proposed use or your institution’s written determination that review is exempt or unnecessary.

After approval, we will email the access instructions. Use the data only for the approved non-commercial project. Obtain permission before redistributing the data and do not attempt to identify participants.

## Citation

You must cite the following paper in any publication that uses PT-3.

Liu, H., Chen, G., Li, Q., Lu, L., Zhang, Y., Xu, Y., Sun, Y., Ning, X., & Yang, X. (2027). [Real-time plantar infrared thermography segmentation: A lightweight deployment-oriented framework and the PT-3 multicenter benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2026.105271). *Information Processing & Management, 64*(2), 105271.
